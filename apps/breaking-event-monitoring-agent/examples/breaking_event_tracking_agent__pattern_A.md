## 📍 INCIDENT I — HIGHEST SIGNIFICANCE
### FAA Telecommunications Failure, Northeast Ground Stops
**Date:** Monday, September 21, 2026

---

### What Happened

At approximately **9:45 a.m. ET** on Monday, September 21, 2026, a primary communications circuit at the **Philadelphia Terminal Radar Approach Control (TRACON)** — the FAA facility that manages radar and radio traffic for multiple Northeast airports — failed. When FAA systems automatically tried to switch to the backup fiber-optic line, crews discovered that the backup line had been **accidentally severed by a construction crew working near New Brunswick, New Jersey**, on or near the Amtrak rail corridor.

The dual failure — primary circuit down, backup cut — left the Philadelphia TRACON without radio and radar capability, forcing the FAA to halt arrivals at **multiple major Northeast airports** for approximately **eight hours**, until approximately **6:00 p.m. ET**, when Secretary of Transportation Sean Duffy confirmed on X that telecom lines had been repaired and operations were resuming.

This was not a United Airlines IT outage. It was an **FAA infrastructure collapse** that struck all carriers operating in the Northeast, but United suffered the most because Newark Liberty International (EWR) is its primary East Coast hub.
