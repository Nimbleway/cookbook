# LLM observability and evaluation platforms landscape (2026-09-23)

Inclusion criteria:
- Dedicated LLM/agent observability or evaluation product, not a general-purpose APM or log-monitoring vendor (Datadog/New Relic/Dynatrace-style platforms excluded even when they ship an LLM module)
- Production-grade LLM/agent tracing and/or evaluation (spans, prompts/responses, tokens, latency, cost, evals, judge metrics)
- Independently identifiable headquarters, leadership, and funding profile (acquired-but-product-continues candidates are included with a note on the acquirer)
- Active development and integration breadth with major LLM providers/agent frameworks (OpenTelemetry-native or SDKs across ≥3 providers)
- Headquartered/incorporated entity that can be the subject of a vendor profile (open-source-only projects without an entity are excluded)
- Self-serve or clearly documented deployment (managed SaaS with sign-up, OSS self-host, or hybrid) — not enterprise sales-only
- At least one publicly documented pricing tier on the vendor's website (free, per-seat, usage-based, or posted enterprise tier)
