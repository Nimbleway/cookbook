## Planted errors — should come back CONTRADICTED (4)

| Claim in the document | Reality |
|---|---|
| Bolt's 9.58 was set at the 2012 London Olympics | Set at the 2009 Berlin World Championships. His London 2012 time was 9.63 — which is why the wrong venue is plausible. |
| Phelps won seven golds at Beijing 2008 | He won eight. Seven is plausible because that was Spitz's Munich total. |
| Brady retired with eight Super Bowl rings | Seven. |
| First modern Olympics: Paris 1896, fourteen nations | Athens 1896. Paris hosted 1900. "Fourteen nations" is the correct figure *for Athens*, so the error is the city, not the count. |

- "nobody has come within a tenth of a second since" — deliberately borderline. Gay and
  Blake have both run 9.69, which is 0.11 off, so the statement is *just* true. A good

## Why sports, and why mostly historical

Every claim above is a settled historical fact, so `time_sensitive` is false and each
one routes through general focus. That matters: `focus: news` returns `url: ""` and is
unusable for citation. The previous fixture leaned on current model pricing and
company facts, which is exactly the material the web settles worst — hence 5 of 10
unverifiable. This subject should cut that sharply.
